The Studio About

Studio Pentire

An authored practice
from Cornwall

A creative studio looking out across the Celtic Sea towards the Atlantic. We make limited edition prints, authored illustration and curated creative resources.

Ground-Mass is the ongoing print series. Each seasonal collection explores abstraction, spatial collage and the landscape of the far west.
