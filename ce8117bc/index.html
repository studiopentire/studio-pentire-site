GAIA Advent Calendar

An eight-SKU seasonal range built around refill and reuse rather than disposal. No additional plastic anywhere: glass and bamboo products inside, paper and card structure outside, and the colour built into through-dyed stocks instead of printed onto them. Fedrigoni Sirio Color Milkshake runs across the calendar and both baubles as the signature stock, with Tintoretto Ceylon, uncoated and felt-marked, as the textured counterpoint. Both FSC certified and elemental-chlorine-free.

The bespoke structures were engineered with cardboard specialists: Think Tank Creative on the baubles, BellaGiada on the calendar.

Bank Street Kitchen

Bank Street Kitchen is an independent café restaurant in Newquay: Italian-inspired, Cornish-made. I worked with the owners from the ground up, on the identity and on how it would live in the room, so the brand and the space were designed as one thing rather than one applied to the other.

The identity centres on a refined serif wordmark with a distinctive K, drawn to flex across a full primary lockup, a compact monogram for small spaces, and a circular version made for a light-box sign over the street. The palette runs warm and earthy: sage, crème, burnt orange and a near-black dark coal, specified to carry the whole way through, from menus and window decals to crockery, signage and the walls. The circular sign is now installed and lit above the door, doing the job it was designed for.

GAIA Product Range

GAIA Skincare's brand and packaging system, rebuilt from the inside. I joined in April 2022 as a Graphic Designer and inherited a range of 50+ SKUs whose packaging had been designed ad hoc over time by several different designers, with no packaging guidelines and no governing system. The result was inconsistent across the range: packaging that didn't hold together or reflect the quality of the products inside.

I took on the repositioning and have owned it continuously since, through my 2025 promotion to Creative Lead across all GAIA brand. The work spans both roles: it began as a designer spotting what was broken, and grew into a brand-wide remit. I redesigned around 80% of the range and added Crystal, SPF, a diffuser, three candles, and bath salts with refills.

The system

I used Optima exclusively across the range, on a palette of GAIA Green, Warm Cream, Mid Stone and Dark Stone.

The system in partner spaces

Visual merchandising guidelines for spa partner retail spaces, written to adapt across a variety of sizes and formats. Rules that hold the brand together in rooms we do not control.

Outcome / proof

The system is still in use today and continues to govern the GAIA brand across the full range, the clearest evidence it was built right. And the remit grew with the work: I joined as a Graphic Designer and was promoted in 2025 to Creative Lead across all of GAIA's brand. The progression is the proof, the business widened the scope because the work earned it.

Reusable aluminium, screen printed

I replaced single-use plastic bottles with refillable aluminium wherever the product allowed, with graphics screen printed directly onto the metal, removing paper labels and adhesives and giving artwork that survives repeated use and washing. The technical heart: no white base, ink on metal, type on the curve.

About

I'm Creative Lead at a Cornish skincare company, where I built the packaging system across a fifty-plus product range and keep it moving as the business grows. Before that, brand and editorial work across beauty, hospitality and publishing.

The work I'm proudest of is where the material carries the idea rather than decorating it: refillable formats, through-dyed stocks, structural packaging engineered with specialists. Sustainability as a design decision, not a sticker.

I work end to end, concept, artwork, supplier, proof, final sample, and I'm as comfortable talking to a printer about board weights as I am presenting a brand. I'm happiest when craft and commercial sense pull in the same direction.
