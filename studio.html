Studio
Pentire

A creative studio looking out across the Celtic Sea towards the Atlantic. We make limited edition prints, authored illustration and curated creative resources.

The work begins with shape, space and the logic of cut-out forms. Each collection explores what happens at the edges of things — where landscape becomes abstraction, where image becomes object.
